Support Independent Software Development

Qixev is an independent software and engineering studio. We build practical developer utilities and open-source tools like Qixev FieldKit — engineered from the ground up without advertisements, telemetry, user tracking, or artificial cloud lock-in.

If our software saves you time or helps your workshop operations, you can voluntarily support our work. Voluntary support helps fund physical testing devices across different Android versions, local build workstations, domain infrastructure, and focused time for open-source maintenance.

Good to know: Support is completely optional. Qixev is an independent commercial studio, not a charity, nonprofit, or NGO, and payments are not tax-deductible donations. Voluntary support is given freely without any expectation of goods, services, or commercial deliverables in exchange — think of it like buying the developer a coffee to back sustainable, independent engineering.
