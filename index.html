Featured Software Product: Qixev FieldKit

An offline workshop inventory and job-tracking Android application designed for independent repair technicians, maintenance engineers, and workshop managers.

FieldKit v1.0.0

- 100% Offline with zero network permissions (no android.permission.INTERNET).
- Generic work units, discrete inventory allocations, stock restitution, and JSON backup/restore.
- Free and open-source under GNU GPL v3.0 or later.
