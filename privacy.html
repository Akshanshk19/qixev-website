Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

1. Static Website Operations

The Qixev website (qixev.studio) is a static website hosted via GitHub Pages.

  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, tracking pixels, session replay tools, advertising beacons, or third-party marketing SDKs.
  • No Cookies: We do not set first-party tracking cookies or third-party cookies.
  • Hosting Server Logs: Standard technical connection logs (such as IP addresses, user agent headers, and request timestamps) may be processed by the hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) in accordance with GitHub's standard infrastructure privacy statements to ensure site security and DDoS mitigation. Qixev does not store, monetize, or profile these server logs.

2. Payments & Voluntary Support

The Qixev website does not embed payment forms or directly collect, process, or store financial credentials, credit/debit card numbers, UPI IDs, or net banking passwords.

  • External Hosted Payment Pages: When users choose to voluntarily support Qixev or settle commercial invoices, they are redirected externally to official hosted payment pages managed by our payment provider (Razorpay).
  • Payment Data Security: Your payment interaction, payment card details, UPI transactions, and banking credentials are handled exclusively on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure and are subject to Razorpay's privacy policy and terms.
  • No Website Payment Gateway: The Qixev website does not run client-side payment gateway SDKs, order creation APIs, or payment verification scripts.
  • Voluntary Support Clarification: Qixev is an independent commercial software studio, not a charity, nonprofit, or NGO. Voluntary support payments are not tax-deductible charitable donations, and there is no expectation of goods or services in exchange.
  • Commercial Invoicing: Contracted client engagements and software development milestones are managed through separate commercial agreements and invoiced independently.
  • Records Retained: Qixev only receives transaction settlement confirmation records (such as transaction IDs, amounts, and dates) necessary for accounting, fulfillment, and statutory tax compliance.

3. Qixev Software (FieldKit)

Software applications published by Qixev, including Qixev FieldKit, are engineered with privacy by design:

  • Zero Network Permissions: The Android application does not include the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It has no physical mechanism to transmit data over the internet.
  • Local Storage: All data entered into FieldKit is saved locally in an SQLite database on your device and remains solely under your control.
  • No Accounts: You do not need an account, email address, or identity verification to use FieldKit.

4. Changes to This Policy

If Qixev introduces new services or infrastructure requiring architectural changes, this document will be updated directly in the public version-controlled repository.

5. Inquiries

For inquiries regarding our software products, services, or privacy practices, email qixevstudios@gmail.com or review our open-source repositories at github.com/Akshanshk19.
